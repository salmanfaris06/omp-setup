# Reference: Landing pages (28 unique designs)

The landing house style is **art-directed and declarative** — one committed canvas (warm paper, naturalistic imagery, or true dark), one confident sentence with a period, one restrained accent, and at least one expected element deliberately missing. Restraint and specificity are the point.

## Canvas families (frequency across 28)
- **Warm paper / cream (`~#EFEDE8`–`#F5F2EA`)**: 9/28 — often with a faint dot-grid. The default light canvas.
- **Full-bleed sky / landscape as canvas**: 7/28 — the most distinctive register. Real photography (wildflower meadow, misty hills, moss macro) or committed painterly/Ghibli illustration. The art is the protagonist at full saturation.
- **Dark (true black / near-black)**: 6/28 — structured by hairline 1px rules (blueprint register), or warmed by a single contained glow (ember horizon, eclipse dome, forest-green + grain).
- **Clinical white**: 6/28. **Flat saturated brand-color canvas**: 1/28 (a committed sky blue with photographic clouds — commitment beats timid tints). **Pure gradient-wash canvases: zero.**

## Imagery strategy (the house signature)
- Nature imagery somewhere on the page: **14/28.** It always has a *designed relationship* to the layout: the page fades INTO a mountain photo at the fold; a forest parts symmetrically around the CTA axis; butterflies float in whitespace; an oil painting sits under floating analytics cards; a translucent product renders over hills. Never a dimmed backdrop behind text.
- Product screenshots appear on 12/28 — but cropped, tilted, fragmented (floating widget cards in clouds, a pastel vignette trio, a glass slab inside a 3D scene), or fused to imagery by a fade. A real terminal shows real command output. **Partiality + real content in the pixels** is what separates it from the fabricated-dashboard tell.
- **Prompt-composer as hero artifact** (a real chat/prompt input with chips, model picker, send circle) recurs 3/28 — a strong current-taste move for AI products: show the *door*, not a fake dashboard.

## Typography
- Grotesque sans base 28/28. **Headlines end with a period: 12/28** — declarative sentences, not title-case fragments.
- Serif appears structurally on 8/28: a whole-serif aphorism headline ("See Further. Think Deeper."), a whole italic-serif *line*, or a fashion-didone display. When mixed with sans it's line-level or chip-level with matched tints and icon chips — **the mid-sentence serif-italic word without that craft is the #1 copied tell; skip it.**
- **Multi-tone headlines: 9/28** — tint 1–2 *semantically chosen* words in a real system color (the value-prop noun), or gray the LEAD line and let the tail line carry black. Never a gradient on the last two words.
- Monospace micro-elements (eyebrows, annotations with directional arrows) on 3/28 — an engineering-register garnish, not a default.

## Hero archetypes (pick by brief, never default)
1. **Centered stack** (15/28): eyebrow → H1 → sub → CTA(s) → artifact. The prevailing rhythm; escape genericness via the artifact (composer, cropped surface, art).
2. **Split copy | art** (8/28): left text with generous whitespace; right full-bleed art panel (iridescent ribbed glass, pixel dither, illustration) touching top and bottom edges.
3. **Image-first, headline-below** (rare, memorable): a huge rounded art block occupying the top ~60%, THEN the serif claim and one black pill.
4. **Omission hero**: no subcopy, no hero CTA — headline + logo row only. Extreme deletion as luxury.
```
(a) centered                 (b) split                    (c) image-first
[ ═ floating pill nav ═ ]    [ ═ nav ═ ]                  [ nav ......... ]
   quiet eyebrow / none      headline. |  full-bleed      ┌───────────────┐
   Declarative headline.     subcopy   |  art panel       │  rounded art  │
   quiet subcopy             [pill]    |  (edge-to-edge)  └───────────────┘
   [pill CTA] (ghost)                  |                   Serif claim.
   [ real artifact, cropped at fold ]                       [black pill]
```

## Nav
- **Floating detached pill capsule** (white, soft-shadowed, 4–5 links): 9/28 — the house nav. Nav is an *object*, not a full-width glass layer.
- Transparent-over-art: 6/28. Segmented pill with an active-item highlight: 3/28. Nav CTA is a pill in ~20/28 — most often **black**.

## CTAs
- Pills ~24/28. **Black pill primary on light canvases: 11/28** — more common than brand-colored.
- **Deliberately small CTAs against a huge canvas: 5/28** — confidence-through-understatement. The oversized h-14 gradient button is the slop twin.
- Dual CTA (solid + ghost) 12/28; single CTA 10/28. Arrow suffixes (→ › ↗) on 10/28; ↗ lives in the warm-neutral register. A pressed/bevel 3D button edge appears twice as a tactile garnish.

## Eyebrows & badges
- 6/28 have **no badge at all** — always an option. When present, the badge carries *real information*: a dated event ("12 July | Free CPE Webinar →"), a partnership, a category; or takes a distinct register: monospace bulleted caps ("• GET PAID FASTER ✳"), em-dash letterspaced caps ("— DESIGN ROOTED IN ESSENCE"), a rectangular outline. The generic "● New — v2.0 →" dot-pill is the tell.

## Gradients & texture
- Gradient usage is **always contained**: a word-level tint (3), an art panel (6), a 1px spectrum rule (1). Zero ambient washes, zero gradient buttons.
- Textures: dot-grid paper (6/28 — drifting toward default; pair with a real art panel or drop), film grain on dark panels (3), halftone/dither pixels (3).

## Page structure below the fold (from the full-page refs)
- **Alternating full-width light/dark bands** rather than endless card grids — a deep forest-green section with grain and a chartreuse accent is the house example. A slim single-line full-width banner CTA; a sticky-left FAQ heading with accordion right; pricing as a 4-col table with ONE lifted column; numbered step cards with giant ghost numerals bleeding off the card edge; a stat trio inline with hairline dividers.
- **No layout family twice.** Logo walls: desaturated gray, small, a separate strip under the hero — never inside it.

## Motion (landing leans scroll + ambient)
- Scroll-reveal + stagger as sections enter; slow drift in contained gradient panels / grain shimmer; floating-card parallax separating a product shot from its photographic backdrop; a traveling dot on an orbit line; marquee logo strips with a "+15 more" pill.
- **Hero product visual: static subtle tilt or a straight floating card — never cursor-following 3D tilt.**

## Premium vs slop (the deltas that matter)
- DO: protagonist imagery with a designed layout relationship; declarative period headlines; black pill CTAs; small-CTA confidence; a real artifact (composer, cropped real surface) instead of a dashboard mock; informative badges or none; one contained grained gradient region max; delete one expected element.
- NEVER: dimmed stock photo behind text · ambient purple/blue glow · gradient text on the whole H1 · the serif-italic word mid-sentence · the "🟢 New" dot-pill · full straight-on fake dashboard with "Welcome back, Alex 👋" · badge+H1+sub+2CTA+avatars+stars with every slot filled · full-width glass nav bar with 7 links · "No credit card required" by reflex.
- Refs that are themselves slop-adjacent (execution references only, not layout gospel): the violet-glow didone dark-SaaS page, the blue-accent analytics template, the italic-serif-chip headline family. Take their craft details (beam texture, bevel button, icon-chip tints), never their skeletons.
