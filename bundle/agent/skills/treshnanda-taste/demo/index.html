Motion doctrine

Alive, never in the way.

The volume of motion comes from a pervasive, non-blocking alive layer. Drama is rationed to rare, earned setpieces. Frequency scales motion: seen often means barely there.

The alive layer

Abundant and tiny. Hover responses, press scale, focus glows, idle float, breathing, gentle gradient drift. Fast, interruptible, off the critical path. This is the secret to alive.
